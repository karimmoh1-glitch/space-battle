How the battle is scored

One number decides a team's place: the highest score it has posted in any single match.

A worked example

Five fictional teams and how they would rank.

RankTeamBestWhy
1Vega Lancers455Highest best score, even with only two matches.
2Titan Forge410Tied at 410 and reached it first, so listed first.
2Ceres Guild410Shares rank 2. No team is placed at rank 3.
4Europa Deep120Rank 4 because two teams share rank 2.
5Nightside Relay-18A negative best still ranks, below every higher score.

When teams tie

Teams with the same best score share a rank and show a TIE marker. The next rank is skipped, giving 1, 1, 3. Within a tie, the team that reached the score first is listed first.

The fine print

Repeat matches
Every result is kept, and only a team's best score sets its rank. A bad match never lowers a team's position.
Team names
Extra spaces and capitalization are ignored, so NOVA SQUAD and Nova Squad are one team. The board shows the latest spelling.
Negative scores
Scores can be below zero and there is no minimum or maximum. A best of -18 ranks above a best of -40.
Corrections
Coordinators can correct or remove a result. The original value is kept with any correction.
