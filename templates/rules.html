Rules

How teams are ranked

Each team is ranked by its best score from a single match. A bad match never lowers a team's rank. The leaderboard also shows each team's total and how many matches it played.

Ties

Teams with the same best score share a rank, and the next rank is skipped.

Other rules

  • Scores are whole numbers and can be negative.
  • Capital letters and extra spaces in team names don't matter. "NOVA SQUAD" and "Nova Squad" are the same team.
  • Tournament staff can fix or delete a result if a mistake is made.
