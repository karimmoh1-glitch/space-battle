Rules

Unless staff say otherwise, each team is ranked by its best score from a single match. A bad match never lowers a team's rank. The leaderboard also shows each team's total, its average, and how many matches it played.

Ranking options

Tournament staff can switch the ranking at any time. The board always says which one is in use.

  • Best single match (default): fair to teams that play fewer matches.
  • Total points: rewards teams that play a lot and score every time.
  • Average per match: rewards being consistent.

Reading the board

  • ▲ and ▼ show a team moving up or down in the last 30 seconds. NEW is a team's first match.
  • A row flashes when that team just finished a match.
  • Latest results lists the last 8 matches, newest first.
  • Click a team's name to see all of its matches and a chart of its scores.
  • When staff lock the results, the board says Final results and no more scores are taken.

Other rules

  • Scores are whole numbers and can be negative.
  • Capital letters and extra spaces in team names don't matter. "NOVA SQUAD" and "Nova Squad" are the same team.
  • Tournament staff can fix or delete a result if a mistake is made.

Questions

Does a bad match hurt my team?

No. Only your best single match decides your rank. Every match still counts toward your total.

What if a score was entered wrong?

Tell the tournament staff. They can fix or delete any result from the admin page.

Our team shows up twice with different spellings. What happens?

Capital letters and spaces are already ignored. For anything else (like "Nova" and "Nova Squad"), staff can merge the two names into one team.
